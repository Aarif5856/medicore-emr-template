MediCoreEMR ADMIN — PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026  ·  Applies to the MediCore EMR Dashboard demo application and website.

Demo application notice: MediCore EMR Dashboard is a user-interface demonstration of a purchasable website template. It is not a real electronic medical records system, is not a medical device, and must not be used to store, process, or manage real patient information.

1. Who we are

MediCore EMR Dashboard ("the App") is published by Mohamed Aarif (Aarif Web Studio). Contact: 5856music@gmail.com.

2. Summary — what we collect

In short: nothing. The App collects no personal data, requires no account, and transmits no information about you to us or to anyone else.
  • No sign-up or account — the login screen is a visual demo only; no credentials are checked, stored, or transmitted.
  • No analytics or tracking — the App contains no analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or tracking pixels.
  • No third-party data sharing — because no data is collected, none is shared or sold.

3. All medical data shown is fictional

Every patient name, medical record, appointment, prescription, invoice, lab result, and statistic displayed in the App is fictional sample data bundled with the application for demonstration purposes. It does not describe real people. Any resemblance to real persons is coincidental.

4. Data stored on your device only

To remember your interface preferences, the App stores the following on your own device using standard browser storage. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

  • medicore-theme — your light/dark mode preference (local storage)
  • medicore-direction — your LTR/RTL layout preference (local storage)
  • sidebar_state — whether the sidebar is expanded or collapsed (cookie, stored locally)

You can clear these at any time from your browser or system settings; the App will simply return to its defaults.

5. Network access

The App requires internet access only to load the application itself (it is delivered as a web application). It makes no background requests, sends no telemetry, and uploads nothing you do inside the demo.

6. Children's privacy

The App is a business/developer demonstration tool, is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Purchased template (source code)

If you purchase the MediCore EMR source-code template, the code you receive contains the same privacy-respecting defaults: no analytics, no tracking, and only the local preference storage described above. If you connect the template to your own backend or add third-party services, you are responsible for the privacy practices of your resulting product.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Because the App collects no data, changes are expected to be rare.
